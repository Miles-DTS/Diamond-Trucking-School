Why Trucking?

Truck drivers are essential!!! Having a career that is in the commercial industry is a desirable career that is in high demand, as well as having job stability. Most truck drivers don't like to call what they do for a living a job, instead they call it a lifestyle.

Many truckers who own their own rig, love adding custom styles and upgrading the interior for maximum living on the road. Many truckers are team drivers as well... Husband and wife, buddies or just teaming up to save money day to day... They enjoy travelling together working and making an adventure!

Interstate Freight

Travel across state lines and see the beauty of North America. From mountains to plains, cities to countryside - experience it all while building your career in the trucking industry.

Intrastate Freight

Discover the diverse landscapes within your home state. From desert highways to rural countryside, local routes offer stability and familiarity while maintaining the adventure of the open road.

Why Drive a Bus?

The bussing industry is always in search of drivers... Bus drivers are beneficial for the transportation service industry. Most bus drivers are employed by various industries of transportation. From Charter Busses to Urban Transit, from School bussing to Services, from Incidental to Transportation.

You can play a vital role as a part-time, full-time or coming out of retirement position. Your career awaits...

What to Expect from Us?

Diamond Trucking School, Inc. will be teaching & guiding you to become a trucker who will practice safety first, defensive driving, handling your rig with driving skills in all weather, from rain to snow, from mountain to countryside, to city to downtown traffic.

You will become comfortable behind the wheel and feel at ease when you're out on the road.
